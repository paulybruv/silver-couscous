How to Play & Rules

🎯 Objective

Name the top 5 Premier League goalscorers for the displayed season. You get 10 points per correct guess, or 5 points if you used a hint. Sweep all 5 cards for a +25 point bonus!

⚠️ Strikes & Hints

  • You are allowed up to 3 strikes per round.
  • Typing a duplicate incorrect guess will not cost an extra strike.
  • Pressing Hint reveals the player's club(s) at the cost of half points.

🤝 The Tie-Breaker System

Historical Premier League seasons often feature players tied on goals. Here is how cards are awarded:

  • Tied for Ranks 1 to 4: All tied players receive their own distinct card with their own individual club hint.
  • Tied for Card #5: When multiple players tie for the final 5th position, they share Card #5. Hitting hint reveals all eligible clubs, and guessing any one of the tied players successfully claims the card with their name!
